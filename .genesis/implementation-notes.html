Known gaps

  1. M3 search ignores required occupancy/hold/min-max-stay truth and does not implement correct total-stay price filtering/sorting/pagination.
  2. M3's complete filters, functional vendor calendar, query-plan/index proof and accessibility evidence are missing; its ISO-string TTL index is ineffective cleanup, though expiry reads remain safe.
  3. Legacy catalog content and 14 images are now staged in MongoDB/ImageKit, but ownership, policies, location, alt text and rights moderation still block publication; the local abstract fallback remains visible meanwhile.
  4. Homepage catalog discovery is client-fetched rather than present in indexable server HTML; the required homepage/detail/calendar/gallery/contact surface remains partial.
  5. No server-side payment or financial truth exists; M5 application persistence, verified real merchant profiles, invoice rendering and customer flow remain incomplete.
  6. Verification/reset deliveries are encrypted and pending; no email/SMS provider or delivery success is claimed before M10.
